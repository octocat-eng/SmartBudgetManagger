# SmartBudgetManagger
A project for managing Budget.
What is it?
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that helps you follow your monthly money. You record your income and expenses, put each expense in a category, and the workbook calculates your balance and shows a chart. If your expenses go over your budget, the status changes to OVER BUDGET.
What do I need?
•	Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible program).
•	The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. No internet and no macros are needed.
How do I start?
Do these steps in order:
1.	Double-click SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If a yellow bar appears at the top, click Enable Editing.
2.	Click the Categories tab (at the bottom) and type your monthly budget for each category.
3.	Click the Transactions tab. In the first empty row, enter the date and a description.
4.	Choose the Type (Income or Expense). For an expense, choose the Category too.
5.	Type the amount in the Income column or in the Expense column, then press Enter.
6.	Click the Dashboard tab to see your balance, the budget status and the chart.
